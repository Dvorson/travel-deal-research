# Stays, packages and ground transport

## Accommodation identity and occupancy

Capture exact property/unit, address, check-in/out, nights, adults, children's ages, bedrooms, actual beds, bathroom and kitchen needs. A sofa bed is not a separate bedroom. Maximum occupancy and eligible child rates are different; Booking documents this distinction [1].

Normalize total stay charges: taxes, cleaning, resort/destination fees, linens, mandatory meals/gala dinners, extra beds, parking, heating, late check-in, cancellation deadline/timezone. Track refundable deposits separately. Missing cost is unknown. A nightly headline without a complete quote is a lead.

Match units across channels using address, operator, unit details and corroborating descriptions. Different rooms in a hotel are not identical products. Separate public, member, mobile, resident and corporate rates, with actual eligibility and membership expense.

## Search experiments

- Compare family room, suite/aparthotel, apartment and two-room configurations where permitted. Verify adult allocation, bedding and connecting rooms rather than assuming them.
- Compare base areas using activity access, terrain, airport and local transport. A rural villa may require a car; a central apartment may eliminate it.
- Discover properties broadly, then compare direct operator and a relevant alternative channel. Direct is not automatically cheapest; compare terms and inclusions.
- Inspect relevant duration boundaries, changeover days, weekly/monthly discounts and minimum stays. Price valid dates; never extrapolate a discounted average. Check altered cancellation/payment terms for long stays.
- Test a split stay when availability or pricing suggests it. Include additional cleaning, transfers, luggage, check-in gaps and time. Confirm whether consecutive bookings retain the same room.
- Compare packages, listing covered components so transfers/bags are not counted twice.
- Model kitchen/meals value only for likely use, and keep spending estimates distinct from quoted charges.
- Add camping cabins, holiday parks, private hostel family rooms, rural tourism or serviced apartments when appropriate. Exchanges, housesitting, work exchange and unnamed-hotel deals are conditional products with different obligations.

## Local discovery

Use tourism directories, local property managers, map business listings, regional portals and native-language searches. Follow business identities to official booking sites. Community posts may reveal a supplier; they do not establish today's availability.

Combine the place, lodging type, party need and direct/official booking term:

- Portugal: `alojamento local`, `apartamento férias`, `turismo rural`, `quinta`, `transfer aeroporto`, `horários`.
- France: `gîte`, `chambre d'hôtes`, `location saisonnière`, `navette aéroport`.
- Italy: `agriturismo`, `casa vacanze`, `affittacamere`.
- Spain: `casa rural`, `apartamento turístico`, `traslado aeropuerto`.
- Germany/Austria: `Ferienwohnung`, `Privatzimmer`, `Familienzimmer`.
- Japan: `公式サイト` (official), `素泊まり` (room only), `家族` (family); verify bedding/meal/child categories.
- Mainland China: `官网` (official), `民宿` (homestay), `亲子` (family), `机场大巴` (airport bus).

Use a registry to corroborate identity, not availability or quality. Never change factual residence, age or eligibility to obtain a rate. Mark inquiry-only rates; draft an inquiry if useful but send only with explicit authorization.

## Transfers and transport

Discover routes, then verify actual-date operator service and party fares. A map does not prove the last bus runs after arrival. Rome2Rio documents estimated prices based on historical/similar routes [2].

Compare public transport, scheduled shuttles, private vehicles, official taxi/rideshare rates, accommodation transfer and car hire. Compare whole-party costs, with luggage, child seats, night/holiday surcharges, pickup and flight-delay waiting terms.

For rail/coach, compare direct operators with useful aggregators and eligible family/group fares. Include seat/berth reservations. Distinguish booking not open from sold out. Validate split-ticket route/time rules and connection protection; UK stopping-station rules are not universal [3]. A pass does not automatically include reservations or save money.

For ferries, check actual sailing date, age categories, pedestrian/vehicle, vehicle dimensions, cabin/seat, port access and check-in cutoff. Verify seasonal routes and disruption notices. Combined sailings can remain separate ticket contracts.

For cars, compare full-trip with selected-day hire. Include pickup/drop-off hours, insurance/excess, refundable deposit, card/driver eligibility, one-way fees, cross-border rules, child seats, parking, fuel and tolls. Relocation rentals suit only flexible, compatible routes; include limits and obligations.

Check time zones, daylight, realistic walking/transfer times, buffers and property arrival windows. Show disruption exposure as qualitative trade-offs or explicit scenarios, not invented probabilities.

## Packages

Map exact inclusions; add only uncovered costs. Match occupancy, airports, bags and board. Read actual seller terms and refresh legal sources before describing statutory protections. For different dates, reprice the independent alternative for those dates too. Never combine prices with an unaccounted night or impossible connection.
